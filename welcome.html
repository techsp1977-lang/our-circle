Getting started

Welcome to Our Circle

Our family's private place for chats, birthdays, photos and a personal journal. Setup takes about 3 minutes.

  1. 1

    Join with your invite link

    Tap the invite link in the text you got. Enter your email, pick a password (7 or more characters), tap Create account, then Join.

    On iPhone, open the link in Safari. If it opened inside another app, look for “Open in Safari”.

  2. 2

    Put it on your Home Screen

    iPhone (Safari) Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add
    Android (Chrome) Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen or Install app

    From now on, open Our Circle from its Home Screen icon. If it asks you to sign in, use the same email and password.

  3. 3

    Turn on notifications

    So you know when family texts you or the whole family.

    Tap ☰ (top right) → My page → Turn on → Allow

    Then tap Send a test and lock your phone. Nothing showed up? iPhone Settings → Notifications → Our Circle → turn on Allow Notifications, Lock Screen and Banners.

    Watch the quick tour
