What it is

Our Circle is a web app for families. It works in the browser on any phone or computer and can be added to your Home Screen like an app. There is nothing to download from an app store.

Family chats

Message everyone, one household, one person or a group, with photos and videos.

Family album

Share photos and videos at full original quality, so anyone can download the originals.

Birthdays & calendar

Birthdays, anniversaries, big days and trips for the whole year, with a nudge on the day.

Staying in touch

Gentle check-in nudges when it's been a while since you messaged a household.

Private journal

Daily pages, gratitude lists and goals, and a printed book of your year.

More than one family

Belong to your own family and your parents' or in-laws' circles, and switch between them.

Plans

Every new family circle gets a 30-day free trial with everything included. No card is needed to start it. When it ends, the circle's organizer chooses a plan. One plan covers everyone in the circle.

Family

$49 / year
  • Everything in Our Circle for the whole family
  • 50 GB for photos and videos (about 15,000 photos)
  • Everyone in the circle joins free

Family Plus

$99 / year
  • Everything in Family
  • 200 GB for photos and videos (about 60,000 photos, or lots of video)
  • For families who share a lot of video

Prices are in US dollars. Journals are private and never count toward the family's storage.

Billing, cancelling and refunds

Yearly billing.
Plans are charged once a year by card through Stripe and renew each year until you cancel.
Access is immediate.
Your plan turns on as soon as the payment goes through.
Cancel anytime.
In Our Circle, go to Family settings, then Plan & billing, then Manage billing. You keep your plan through the end of the year you paid for.
Changing plans.
You can move from Family to Family Plus at any time and pay only the difference for the rest of your year.
If a plan ends or storage is full.
Only new photo and video uploads pause. Messages, the calendar, journals and every photo already shared keep working, and originals can still be downloaded.
Refunds.
If you were charged by mistake or have a problem with a charge, email us within 30 days and we'll make it right.

Privacy

What we keep.
Your sign-in email and name, and what your family puts in Our Circle: names, photos and birthdays of family members (including profiles parents create for young children), messages, shared photos and videos, dates and trips, and your own journal pages and goals.
Who can see it.
Everything in a family circle is visible only to the people in that circle. Your journal and goals are visible only to you in the app; no one else in your family can see them.
Lock your journal.
You can lock your journal with a journal password (on the Journal page, tap Lock my journal). Your pages and goals are then encrypted on your phone before they're saved, so no one else can read them, not even the people who run Our Circle. You get a recovery code in case you forget the password. If you lose both the password and the recovery code, the locked pages can't be recovered by anyone. You can start over with an empty journal.
Journals that aren't locked.
They're private to you in the app and stored on Google's servers like everything else. The people who run Our Circle could technically reach that storage, but we don't read journals. Locking your journal closes that gap.
Where it lives.
Our Circle is stored with Google Firebase in the United States.
Payments.
Cards are handled by Stripe. Our Circle never sees or stores your card number.
Notifications.
If you turn on notifications, new-message previews are delivered through Apple's, Google's or your browser's push service. You can turn them off on My page or in your phone's settings.
Face ID.
Face ID sign-in uses a passkey that stays on your phone. We keep only a public key that can't be used to sign in anywhere else. Your face data never leaves your phone.
No selling.
We don't sell your information or show ads.
Download or delete.
You can download the original of any shared photo or video at any time. To delete your account, or a whole family circle and everything in it, email us and we'll do it within 30 days.
